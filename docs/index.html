Aero

A small CSS animation library with an optional 7 KB script. Add a class, get the motion. It looks the same in every modern browser.

Draw

aero-draw draws an SVG stroke in. Put it on a shape, or on the <svg> to draw every shape inside. aero.js sets pathLength="1" on the shapes if it is missing (without the script, add it yourself). It animates stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset, the one exception to the transform and opacity rule. It needs a stroke, and works as an entrance or with aero-scrub.

<svg class="aero-enter aero-draw" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="4">
  <path d="M8 70 C 50 10, 90 10, 130 70"/>
</svg>
<svg class="aero-scrub aero-draw" style="--aero-scrub-range: cover 0% cover 60%">...</svg>

Scroll-linked

Add aero-scrub to any variant and the animation follows the scroll position instead of time. --aero-scrub-range reads like animation-range. The same markup works in every browser: where CSS scroll timelines exist they run it, and elsewhere aero.js drives the identical animation.

The bar at the top of this page is aero-scrub aero-scrub-root with its own scaleX keyframes.

<div class="aero-scrub aero-fade-up" style="--aero-scrub-range: entry 0% entry 80%">...</div>
<div class="aero-scrub aero-pass" style="--aero-scrub-range: cover 0% cover 100%">...</div>
<div class="aero-scrub aero-fan-left" style="--aero-scrub-range: entry 25% cover 50%">...</div>

Depth

aero-depth is scroll parallax: write aero-scrub aero-depth and the element moves against the scroll as it passes, from --aero-depth (80px) below its place to the same distance above. A negative value reverses it. The range defaults to cover 0% cover 100%.

Slide and fade out

aero-slide-left and aero-slide-right only make sense tied to the scroll, so use them with aero-scrub. A row of words slides sideways across its range, by --aero-slide (30% of its own width) from --aero-slide-start (0). aero-fade-out fades to --aero-opacity-to (0), and aero-fade-in starts from --aero-opacity-from (0).

Underline

aero-underline draws a link's underline in from the left on hover or focus, and out to the right when you leave. It replaces the link's own underline. Settings: --aero-underline-color -thickness -offset.

Cursor

aero.js tracks the pointer over any aero-cursor element and writes --aero-cursor-x, -y, -cx, -cy and -active. Effects inside it read those. data-aero-smoothing sets how far the values lag behind the pointer. Move your mouse over the panel.

aero-tilt

Tilts toward the pointer.

aero-spotlight

A soft light follows the pointer.

Marquee

aero-marquee on a container whose single child is the track: the track scrolls left forever and loops seamlessly. aero.js copies the items once (the copies are hidden from screen readers), so the items together should be wider than the container, and spaced with padding or margin rather than gap. Hover or focus pauses it. --aero-marquee-duration (30s) sets one full loop; aero-marquee-reverse goes the other way.

Disclosure

aero-collapse on a <details> slides its content open and closed (Chrome and Edge today; elsewhere it just opens). aero-flip turns a two-faced card over on hover, on focus inside, or when you add aero-flipped. Hover alone does not flip on touch screens. An aero-collapse-icon in the summary, such as a "+", turns by --aero-collapse-turn (45deg) when it opens, as the first one does.

What does the collapse do? +

The content opens and closes with a block-size transition. The library never sets interpolate-size on :root; it is set on the element.

Can I change the speed?

Yes: --aero-duration and --aero-easing.

Between pages

Three steps give you cross-page transitions. Opt in to view transitions in your CSS, load aero-pages.js in the <head> of every page (not deferred), and mark the pages that sit on top of others. Sheets slide in from the right over the last page and slide away on the way back. Firefox gets the same direction from aero-arrive.

/* 1. your CSS */
@view-transition { navigation: auto; }

<!-- 2. in the head of every page -->
<script src="aero-pages.js"></script>

<!-- 3. on pages that sit on top -->
<html lang="en" data-aero-page="sheet">

Edge

An aero-edge panel is fixed at the left edge of the page. As a mouse pointer nears the edge (within --aero-edge-zone, 220px) aero.js eases --aero-edge from 0 to 1 on <html>, and the panel widens to --aero-edge-reach (24vw; set on :root, here 280px). Inside it, aero-edge-drift drifts in from the left and aero-edge-fade fades in late. aero-edge-shift on the page's blocks slides them aside with it. Mouse only, and it stays shut while a button is held, a dialog is open or text is selected.

It is live on this page: move your mouse to the left edge. The nav, the sections and the footer slide aside, and the panel takes clicks once it is open.

Clicking the panel shows a message here.

Reduced motion

Motion plays by default, even when the visitor has turned on "Reduce motion", because many people use that setting to calm the operating system and not to switch off a site. To respect it, add aero-reduce-motion to <html>. Visitors who prefer reduced motion then see everything at once and still, and aero.js stops driving anything.

Off. Reduced motion has no effect on this page.

The class only has an effect if your operating system is set to prefer reduced motion. Otherwise toggling it changes nothing.

<html lang="en" class="aero-reduce-motion">
